About

Dr. Carlos A. Theran-Suarez is an Assistant Professor of Computer and Information Sciences at Florida A&M University. His work integrates machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, remote sensing, natural language processing, and scalable computing to develop data-driven solutions for healthcare and other high-impact domains.

His research interests include medical image analysis, neurodegenerative disease assessment, cancer detection, multimodal data fusion, explainable AI, trustworthy model evaluation, and cloud-based AI infrastructure.

Education

  • Ph.D., Computer and Information Science and Engineering, University of Puerto Rico at Mayagüez
  • M.S., Scientific Computing, University of Puerto Rico at Mayagüez
  • B.S., Mathematical Sciences, Universidad del Atlántico

Research Laboratory

Intelligent Imaging and Health Analytics Lab

The IIHA Lab develops intelligent imaging, machine learning, and multimodal analytics methods that transform complex biomedical, clinical, geospatial, sensor, and textual data into interpretable and actionable knowledge.

01

Medical Imaging

MRI, CT, low-dose CT, histopathology, image classification, segmentation, and explainable computer vision.

02

Healthcare Analytics

AI methods for neurodegenerative diseases, cancer, diabetes, clinical risk modeling, and decision support.

03

Multimodal Intelligence

Fusion of imaging, clinical, sensor, remote-sensing, and language data using modern neural architectures.

04

Trustworthy AI

Explainability, model evaluation, bias assessment, LLM observability, reproducibility, and responsible deployment.

Research Opportunities

Motivated undergraduate and graduate students interested in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, healthcare AI, remote sensing, data science, or scalable computing are encouraged to inquire about available projects.

Recommended preparation

  • Python programming and data structures
  • Foundations in probability, statistics, and linear algebra
  • Machine learning or deep learning coursework
  • Experience with Git and reproducible experimentation
  • Commitment to research documentation and teamwork

Student inquiry checklist

Include the following in your email:

  1. Degree program and expected graduation date
  2. Research interests and preferred project area
  3. Relevant coursework and technical experience
  4. Résumé or CV
  5. GitHub, portfolio, or prior work, when available
  6. Weekly availability and intended research period
